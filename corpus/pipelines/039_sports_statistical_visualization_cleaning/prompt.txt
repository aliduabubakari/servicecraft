# Independent sports summaries converging into one report

1. Objective

Build a pure fan-in graph. Three independent root tasks compute team production, position play/shooting, and four age-band summaries directly from the input. A final task consumes all three summaries, reconciles their player totals, and reports summary counts and the highest-scoring team. The branches must not depend on one another.

2. Available input files

- inputs/data.csv
  Columns present: player_id, team, position, games_played, points_per_game, assists_per_game, rebounds_per_game, minutes_per_game, age, experience_years, salary_millions, field_goal_pct, three_point_pct, free_throw_pct

These are available source columns, not a requirement to use every column. Follow the task-specific column-selection and validation rules below. Input paths are relative to the supplied generation workspace.

3. Required processing behavior

Apply all processing requirements in the objective above. Retain its specified transformations, parameters, validation policies and ordering.

4. Dependency and topology requirements

Logical task counts stated in the requirements describe required operations; any permitted alternative Dagster decomposition must preserve their behavior, topology and observable checkpoints.

5. Mandatory output artifacts

- `position_summary.csv`
