# Shared player features feeding two models and a joint report

1. Objective

Build a nine-task fan-in/fan-out graph. Validate players; independently derive performance features (production and shooting indices) and availability/context features; join them one-to-one. Fan the joined table to market-value Ridge regression (80/20 seed-42 split, alpha=1, train-only scaling) and All-Star logistic classification (stratified 75/25 seed-42 split, C=1, liblinear, threshold 0.5, train-only scaling). Evaluate regression with MAE/RMSE/R2 and classification with accuracy/precision/recall/F1/ROC AUC, then reconverge both metric artifacts into a joint report.
