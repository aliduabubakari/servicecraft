# Transportation economics with convergent feature branches

1. Objective

Build a `fan_in_fan_out` trip-economics DAG. Validate unique trip_id, finite nonnegative measures, and emit trips_clean.csv. It fans out into revenue features (revenue_per_passenger and revenue minus fare*passengers) and cost features (cost_per_km and cost_per_delay_minute, zero for zero delay). Join branches one-to-one with clean trips, derive profit=revenue-cost and profit_margin=profit/revenue, and emit trip_economics.csv.

2. Available input files

- inputs/data.csv
  Columns present: trip_id, route, vehicle_type, departure_hour, distance_km, passengers, fare_amount, delay_minutes, day_of_week, is_holiday, weather_condition, revenue, operational_cost

These are available source columns, not a requirement to use every column. Follow the task-specific column-selection and validation rules below. Input paths are relative to the supplied generation workspace.

3. Required processing behavior

The integrated table fans out to a profit regression and route profitability summary. Regression uses ten ordered predictors, 80/20 split seed 42, train-only StandardScaler and LinearRegression; emits model/predictions and MAE/RMSE/R2 metrics. Route summary emits counts, total revenue/cost/profit and mean margin. economics_report.json reconverges metrics and summary, publishes global totals and most profitable route with route ascending tie-break.

4. Dependency and topology requirements

Preserve the logical ordering, data dependencies, independent branches and joins specified in the objective and processing requirements. Independent branches must not acquire dependencies on one another; joins must consume the required current-run upstream artifacts. Do not replace a required branched pipeline with a sequential chain or add a join between independent components. Logical task counts stated in the requirements describe required operations; any permitted alternative Dagster decomposition must preserve their behavior, topology and observable checkpoints.
