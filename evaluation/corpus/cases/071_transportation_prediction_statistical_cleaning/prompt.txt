# Parallel delay classification and route service analysis

1. Objective

Build two disconnected three-task components. The delay component prepares declared numeric and categorical predictors, uses a fixed stratified 75/25 seed-42 split, train-only numeric scaling and categorical one-hot encoding, LogisticRegression (C=1, liblinear), threshold 0.5, and five classification metrics. The service component independently prepares trip service fields, aggregates route delay/on-time/satisfaction plus service_score=(on_time+satisfaction/5)/2, and reports totals and the best route. Neither component consumes artifacts from the other.

- inputs/data.csv
